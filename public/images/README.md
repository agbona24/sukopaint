# Product Images

Place your paint product images in this directory with the following names:

- `emulsion.jpg` - Emulsion paint product image
- `satin.jpg` - Satin paint product image
- `matt.jpg` - Matt paint product image
- `gloss.jpg` - Gloss paint product image
- `silk.jpg` - Silk paint product image

## Image Requirements

- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Recommended size**: 800x800 pixels (1:1 aspect ratio)
- **File size**: Keep under 500KB for optimal performance
- **Background**: White or transparent background works best

## Current Status

Currently, the website will show gradient backgrounds with animated icons as placeholders until you add your actual product images. Once you add the images with the correct filenames, they will automatically appear on the website.
